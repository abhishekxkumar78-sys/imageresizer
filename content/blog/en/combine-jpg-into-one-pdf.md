When handling paperwork online, you often collect multiple individual pictures instead of a single document. Multi-page agreements, certificates, receipts for expense reports, or notes usually need to be submitted together. Some forms and portals accept only one file rather than separate image uploads.

Assembling those pictures into a structured PDF document keeps every page in sequence and simplifies sharing. Our browser tool lets you arrange images in any order, choose page orientation and margins, and produce an A4 PDF without installing software.

## How to combine JPG images into one PDF

Follow these 6 steps to convert your JPG pictures into a PDF document:

1. Open the [Image to PDF](https://www.freeeimageresizer.com/image-to-pdf) tool page in your browser.
2. Click Select Images or PDFs, or drag and drop your files into the upload area. You can select several images at once.
3. Review the queue below the settings panel. Each image appears with a preview and a Page N label showing its initial position.
4. Adjust the sequence using the drag handle on the left of each card, or click the arrow buttons. To remove an image, click the ✕ button.
5. Set your layout under Page Orientation (Auto (Fit Image Aspect Ratio), Portrait (Standard A4 Vertical), or Landscape (Horizontal)) and Page Margins (No Margin (Full Page Fit) or Small Margin (10mm Padding)).
6. Click Convert to PDF & Download to generate your file. The browser compiles your pages and saves converted-images.pdf to your device.

## Getting the order right

File names from a camera, a phone or a scanner do not always follow the order of your document. In our tool, the final PDF pages follow the exact vertical order shown in the queue. Each card displays Page 1, Page 2, Page 3, and so on, updating immediately whenever you move an item.

To rearrange items, drag the dotted handle on the left of any card (labeled Drag to reorder), or click the Move up (↑) and Move down (↓) arrow buttons on the right. The top item has its up arrow disabled, and the bottom item has its down arrow disabled.

To remove an unwanted file, click the ✕ button on that card (labeled Remove). There is no Clear all button; items are removed one by one. To append forgotten pictures, click the + Add More Files button without clearing your list.

## Orientation and margins

Every page uses standard A4 paper dimensions (210 by 297 millimeters). The tool focuses exclusively on A4 without Letter or Legal options.

Under Page Orientation, choose between three settings:

* Auto (Fit Image Aspect Ratio): The default setting. The tool inspects each image. Wide pictures become landscape A4 pages (297 by 210 mm), while tall pictures become portrait A4 pages (210 by 297 mm). This per-page calculation lets you combine portrait forms and landscape charts in one document.
* Portrait (Standard A4 Vertical): Sets every page to vertical A4.
* Landscape (Horizontal): Sets every page to horizontal A4.

Under Page Margins, select how the image sits on the sheet:

* No Margin (Full Page Fit): Scales the picture to fit the A4 page while preserving its aspect ratio. The image is never stretched and never cropped. Because standard photo dimensions differ from A4 proportions, empty white bands remain along edges where the image does not reach the border. The picture sits centered.
* Small Margin (10mm Padding): Adds a 10 mm border on all sides, scaling the image to fit centered within the inner area.

## Keeping the PDF small

Photos from a phone can be large. Combining many large pictures can produce a PDF that is bigger than an email or a portal accepts.

Our tool includes an optional Target PDF Size (Optional) setting with KB and MB units. When left empty, images embed using original data without recompressing. Entering a target value adjusts JPEG compression quality through automated test passes. If the file remains over 15 percent above the target, the tool reduces the pixel dimensions step by step; if unreachable, it gives the closest size achieved.
