About ImageResizer

Fast, private, free image processing — entirely in your browser.

Our Mission

ImageResizer was built to solve a simple problem: resizing and cropping images online should be fast, private, and free. Too many tools require account signups, upload your images to remote servers, or slap watermarks on your downloads. We set out to build something better.

Every image you process with ImageResizer stays on your device. We use the HTML5 Canvas API to handle all resizing, cropping, and format conversion directly in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server — not even temporarily.

Why We Built This

As developers and designers, we found ourselves repeatedly needing a quick way to resize images without launching heavy software like Photoshop or GIMP. Existing online tools often felt slow, cluttered with ads, or required uploading sensitive images to unknown servers.

ImageResizer is our answer: a lightweight, focused tool that respects your privacy and gets out of your way. No signups, no uploads, no limits — just drag, resize, and download.

Our Values

Privacy First

Your images never leave your device. All processing happens client-side, so your data stays yours.

Blazing Fast

No server round-trips means near-instant processing. Resize an image in milliseconds, not seconds.

Completely Free

No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no watermarks. ImageResizer is and always will be free to use.

Simple by Design

Clean, distraction-free interface that lets you focus on your images — not on figuring out how the tool works.
