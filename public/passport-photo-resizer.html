Global Passport Photo Size Requirements Reference

Official visa and passport agencies require precise millimeter or inch dimensions with minimum DPI (dots per inch) standards. Below are standard sizes for popular passports:

US Passport & Visa

2 × 2 inches (600×600 px)

51 × 51 mm at 300 DPI. Square format used for US passports, Green Card applications, and US visas.

UK / EU / Schengen

35 × 45 mm (413×531 px)

Standard European passport size used across Schengen countries, UK, Australia, and New Zealand.

India Passport / OCI

2 × 2 in or 35 × 45 mm

Indian passport renewals require 2x2 inches (51x51mm), while Pan Card / Driving Licenses use 35x45mm.

DV Lottery (Green Card)

600 × 600 px (Max 240KB)

Strict 1:1 square digital submission requirements for the US Diversity Visa lottery program.

How to Resize Your Photo to Passport Specifications

  1. Take a clear headshot: Stand against a plain white or light background in bright, even lighting.
  2. Crop your portrait: Open our Crop Tool to frame your face from top of hair to bottom of chin centered.
  3. Set Pixel Dimensions: Enter target pixel dimensions (e.g. 600 x 600 for US passport or 413 x 531 for UK/Schengen).
  4. Export & Print: Download your resized photo as a high-quality JPG file ready for online submission or 4x6 photo sheet printing.

Official Guidelines for Passport & ID Photos

  • Neutral Facial Expression: Look straight at the camera with eyes open and mouth closed. No smiling or frowning.
  • No Eyeglasses or Headwear: Unless worn daily for religious or medical reasons, eyeglasses and hats should be removed.
  • Even Background: Ensure there are no dark shadows behind your head or on your face.
