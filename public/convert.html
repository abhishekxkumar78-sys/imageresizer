What can this converter do?

ImageResizer's converter changes the format of images, audio and video inside your browser without installing software. Convert images from PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, or AVIF into PNG, JPG, or WebP. Convert audio tracks from MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC, or OPUS into MP3 or WAV, and convert videos from MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, AVI, or M4V into MP4 or WebM. Your files are processed entirely on your machine and are never uploaded to a server.

How it works

  1. Choose or drop a file from your device.
  2. Pick the output format and adjust quality or bitrate settings.
  3. Click Convert, then download the result.

Conversions run locally on your device using web standards. Images are processed with your browser's Canvas. Audio is encoded in a background Web Worker using lamejs for MP3 and raw PCM for WAV. Video is converted by FFmpeg running as WebAssembly. The video engine is downloaded only when you start a video conversion, which is a download of about 31 MB that happens for the first time only.

Limits

There is no fixed size limit for images, but very large images use a lot of browser memory and may fail on low-memory devices. Audio up to 50 MB and 10 minutes. Video up to 100 MB, 5 minutes and 1080p. Because video conversion runs on your device rather than a remote cloud server, processing large files depends on your device speed and memory, so keep the tab open until it finishes.

Frequently asked questions

Is the converter free and do you upload my files?

Yes, the converter is completely free to use with no account registration, subscriptions, or watermarks. All processing happens locally in your browser, so your files are never uploaded, stored, or inspected by a remote server.

Which formats can I convert?

You can import images in PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP, and AVIF formats and save them as PNG, JPG, or WebP. You can import audio in MP3, WAV, OGG, M4A, FLAC, and OPUS formats and save them as MP3 or WAV. You can import video in MP4, WebM, MOV, MKV, AVI, and M4V formats and save them as MP4 or WebM.

Is there a file size limit?

There is no fixed size limit for images, but very large images use a lot of browser memory and may fail on low-memory devices. Audio up to 50 MB and 10 minutes. Video up to 100 MB, 5 minutes and 1080p.

Will converting reduce quality?

Converting to PNG preserves lossless image quality, while converting to JPG or WebP is lossy and depends on the quality level you select. When converting images with transparent backgrounds to JPG, transparent areas are automatically filled with solid white.

Why is video conversion slower than images?

Video processing requires substantial computation to decode and encode every frame. Because encoding runs entirely on your device using WebAssembly instead of remote cloud servers, speed depends on your hardware. The video engine also downloads only on your first video conversion (about 31 MB), for the first time only.
