Official Instagram Image Dimensions (2026 Cheat Sheet)

Posting photos with wrong aspect ratios causes Instagram to automatically crop or heavily compress your images, making them blurry. To ensure your posts remain sharp and perfectly framed, use the recommended pixel dimensions below:

Square Post (1:1)

1080 × 1080 px

Standard Instagram feed posts. Ideal for balanced compositions, product highlights, and quick updates.

Portrait Post (4:5)

1080 × 1350 px

Occupies the most vertical screen real estate in mobile feeds, delivering maximum visibility and engagement.

Stories & Reels (9:16)

1080 × 1920 px

Full-screen vertical format for Stories, Reels, and IGTV cover photos.

Landscape Post (1.91:1)

1080 × 566 px

Best for panoramic photos, wide landscape photography, and horizontal banner visuals.

Pro Tips to Prevent Blurry Instagram Photos

  • Upload in JPG or PNG format: Instagram automatically converts PNGs to JPG, but uploading a clean 1080px JPG reduces compression artifacts.
  • Do not upload oversized images (>25MB): Very large files undergo aggressive server-side compression by Instagram. Pre-resizing to 1080px width avoids this.
  • Keep main focal points centered: Profile feed grids display thumbnails as 1:1 squares, regardless of whether the original post was portrait or landscape.
