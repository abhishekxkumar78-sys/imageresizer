Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2025

Our Commitment to Your Privacy

At ImageResizer, we take your privacy seriously. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and services.

1. No Image Uploads

Your images never leave your device. All image processing — resizing, cropping, format conversion, and compression — is performed entirely in your browser using the HTML5 Canvas API. We do not upload, store, transmit, or have any access to the images you process.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information necessary to operate and improve our service:

  • Anonymous usage data — We may collect basic, non-personally-identifiable analytics about page visits (e.g., which pages are visited, browser type, device type) to help us improve the user experience.
  • No personal information — We do not require registration, accounts, or email signups. We do not collect your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

3. Cookies

We use minimal cookies necessary for basic website functionality. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies. You can configure your browser to reject cookies, but some parts of the site may not function properly as a result.

4. Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Fonts — We use the Inter font served by Google Fonts. Your browser may communicate with Google's servers to download the font files. See Google's Privacy Policy for details.

5. Data Security

Since your images never leave your device, there is no risk of your image data being intercepted, stored, or accessed by us or any third party during processing. Our website is served over HTTPS to ensure secure communication between your browser and our server.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
